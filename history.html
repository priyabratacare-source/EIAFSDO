Chapter 01 · The Spark

When the Hackathon Changed Everything

It started with an announcement poster on our college notice board. Smart India Hackathon (SIH) was organizing — and suddenly, the three of us had a thousand questions.

// Chapter 01

Three friends, one poster, endless questions

📌 ❓ 🤔 💭 ⚡

We were in the middle of our regular college days — classes, assignments, and the usual "we'll build something someday" conversations. Then SIH was announced at our college, and everything shifted. Three of us — Priyabrata Patra, Abhradeep Dhara, and Ayush Chatterjee — sat together staring at that poster and started asking questions we didn't have answers to.

"Can we even participate?" · "We only know a little." · "How do people build full-stack apps?" · "Can we use AI to help us?" · "Is our knowledge enough?"

"We had more questions than skills. But that day, the questions themselves became the beginning of everything."

Instead of giving up, we did what any curious college student does in 2026 — we opened ChatGPT, Gemini, and every AI tool we could find, and started learning in public. The AI didn't give us an organization. But it gave us confidence. And confidence, it turns out, is the seed of a movement.

Chapter 02 · The Group

One WhatsApp Group. Named "For Web Making & Discussion"

Late at night, after the college day ended, we created a group — not knowing it would one day become an organization.

// Chapter 02

The group where the actual work happened

📱 💬 🌙 ☕ 🔥

Every serious project starts in a chat window at 11:42 PM. Ours did too. The group was called "For Web Making and discussion" — a name so simple it barely sounded like a name at all. It was just three friends sharing YouTube links, screenshots of errors, and late-night voice notes about what to learn next.

We discussed everything in that group — from "what is Firebase?" to "how do APIs actually work?" to "should we build a real platform someday?". Slowly, the group stopped being a group and started becoming a team.

"The group had no rules, no leader, no plan. Just three friends who refused to stop asking questions."

That chat eventually became the birthplace of something much bigger — the foundation of the community we now call EIAFSDO.

Chapter 03 · The Build

How We Actually Built the Platform

No funding. No team. No senior mentor. Just three co-founders, four pillars of engineering, and a lot of determination.

🌐

Frontend & Web Design

Every page was hand-built from scratch — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. From the warm neo-brutalist aesthetic to the animated login background, the design language was crafted to feel personal, warm, and human.

🗄️

Backend & Database

Powered by Firebase — Firestore for real-time data, Authentication for secure login, and structured collections for users, projects, talks, messages, and activity logs. No SQL servers, just clean cloud-native backend.

🔐

Security & Access Control

Role-based access — admins, super-admins, and members each see different sections. Self-review protection, admin-only panels, password reset restrictions, and super-admin privileges are all handled at the logic level.

🤖

AI-Assisted Learning

We didn't have seniors. We had AI. Every bug, every architecture question, every "how does this even work?" was answered using ChatGPT, Gemini, and a lot of trial and error. AI became our first mentor.

// What We Learned

The lessons we didn't expect

📚 🧠 💪 ❤️ 🌟

We learned that you don't need to know everything to start. We learned that asking "dumb" questions is not a weakness — it's the beginning of wisdom. We learned that AI is not a replacement for learning; it's a companion for the journey.

We learned that a WhatsApp group with no rules can build something real. And most importantly — we learned that three co-founders who refuse to give up are stronger than any single expert.

"From a poster on a college wall to a full-stack platform — every step was taken together."

This page will keep growing as EIAFSDO grows. More chapters, more milestones, more co-founders, more community members. The story is being written live.
