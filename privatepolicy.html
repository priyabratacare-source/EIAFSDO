Part 01

Privacy Policy — Organization

1.1 Introduction

The East Indian Amateur Full-Stack Developers Organization (“EIAFSDO,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) is a student-operated collective based in Tarakeswar, Hooghly, West Bengal, India. We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our members, volunteers, website visitors, and all individuals who interact with our organization.

This Privacy Policy describes how EIAFSDO collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal data in the course of our organizational activities. It is published in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 (“IT Act”), the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (“SPDI Rules”), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”).

By becoming a member of EIAFSDO, participating in our activities, or interacting with our organizational platforms, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1.2 Who This Policy Applies To

  • Registered Members of EIAFSDO
  • Founding Board Members, Chairman, CEO, and other leadership roles
  • Volunteers and contributors
  • Event Participants — workshops, hackathons, talks
  • Website Users and visitors
  • Third Parties who interact with EIAFSDO in an official capacity

1.3 Personal Data We Collect

EIAFSDO collects the following categories of personal data from members and participants:

⚠️
Note on Sensitive Personal Data

EIAFSDO does not ordinarily collect Sensitive Personal Data or Information (SPDI) as defined under the SPDI Rules — such as financial information, health data, sexual orientation, or biometric information. Passwords are collected solely for authentication and stored in hashed form through Firebase Authentication.

1.4 Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data on the following legal bases:

  1. Consent — You provide explicit consent when registering as a member or using our platforms.
  2. Contractual Necessity — Processing is necessary to fulfill our obligations as an organization to our members.
  3. Legitimate Interests — For organizational administration, governance, and community management.
  4. Legal Compliance — Where required by applicable Indian law.

1.5 How We Use Personal Data

  • Member Administration — Managing membership records, roles, and organizational structure
  • Governance — Board decisions, role appointments, and organizational records
  • Platform Operation — Operating the dashboard, digital ID cards, and notice systems
  • Communication — Sending organizational notices, updates, and announcements
  • Project Management — Reviewing, approving, and showcasing member projects
  • Community Building — Facilitating talks, discussions, and collaboration
  • Verification — Confirming member identity and roles within the organization
  • Legal Compliance — Meeting statutory obligations under Indian law

1.6 Organizational Data Sharing

EIAFSDO does not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We may share data in the following limited circumstances:

  • Within the Organization — Board members, Chairman, CEO, and authorized administrators may access member data for governance and administration purposes.
  • Public-Facing Areas — Member names, photographs, roles, bios, and developer links may be displayed on the public team page and verified profile modals.
  • Service Providers — We use Firebase (Google) for authentication and data storage. These providers process data under their own terms and applicable data protection laws.
  • Legal Requirements — We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

1.7 Data Retention

  • Active Membership Period — Personal data is retained for the duration of active membership, plus a reasonable period thereafter for archival and governance purposes.
  • Organizational Records — Board decisions, notices, and appointments are retained as historical records.
  • Deletion Requests — User account data may be deleted upon request, subject to organizational record-keeping requirements.

1.8 Security Practices

  • Firebase Authentication — Passwords are never stored in plain text; authentication is handled by Google Firebase with industry-standard encryption.
  • Firestore Security Rules — Database access is controlled by role-based security rules.
  • Role-Based Access Control — Member data is accessible only to authorized roles.
  • HTTPS Encryption — All data transmission is encrypted.
  • Regular Review — Organizational security practices are reviewed periodically.

1.9 Member Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection laws, members have the right to:

  • Access their personal data held by EIAFSDO
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of their data (subject to organizational requirements)
  • Withdraw consent (which may affect membership status)
  • Lodge a grievance with the EIAFSDO Board

1.10 Contact for Organizational Privacy

Issuing Authority: EIAFSDO Board

Address: Tarakeswar, Hooghly, West Bengal, India

1.11 Changes to This Policy

EIAFSDO may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the EIAFSDO notice board, email to registered members, and a notice on the organization website.
