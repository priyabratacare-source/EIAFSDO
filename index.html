Who We Are

About EIAFSDO

We're a student‑run community of aspiring full‑stack developers from East India, driven by curiosity and a passion for craftsmanship.

East Indian Amateur Full-Stack Developers Organization (EIAFSDO) is a student‑operated organization, sprouted out of a band of interested students of HETC, who wanted to explore the craftsmanship behind the web apps and the mobile apps we use daily.

Our motto is “Aedificare — Disputare — Crescere” (Build · Discuss · Grow). We believe in learning by building, growing through discussion, and evolving as a community.

From DevConnect to EduTrack, every project we create is a stepping stone toward becoming better engineers — and a way to give back to the developer community.
