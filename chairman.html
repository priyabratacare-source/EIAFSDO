A Message for EIAFSDO

CHAIRMAN'S MESSAGE

Build with curiosity. Learn through practice. Grow together.

EIAFSDO is built around the idea that technology becomes meaningful when people learn, experiment and create together. Every project, discussion and development activity gives us an opportunity to understand something new and turn that learning into practical work. Our focus should remain on building useful things, supporting one another, documenting what we learn and creating an environment where new ideas can become real projects. As we continue developing EIAFSDO, teamwork, responsible technology and continuous learning should remain important parts of our journey.
Abhradeep Dhara
