01 / PROFILE

About Ayush Chatterjee

A founding team profile within the East Indian Amateur Full-Stack Developers Organization.

Ayush Chatterjee is a member of the founding team of the East Indian Amateur Full-Stack Developers Organization , commonly known as EIAFSDO.

His name is part of the organization's founding structure alongside Priyabrata Patra and Abhradeep Dhara .

The founding team represents the starting organizational structure from which EIAFSDO's technology, development, project and collaboration activities are organized.

This page provides an official-style digital profile for Ayush Chatterjee within the EIAFSDO website structure.
