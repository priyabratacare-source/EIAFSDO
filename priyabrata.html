01 / PROFILE

About Priyabrata Patra

A technology-focused profile spanning organizational administration, web development, IT systems and independent technology entrepreneurship.

Priyabrata Patra is a member of the founding team of the East Indian Amateur Full-Stack Developers Organization , known as EIAFSDO.

Within EIAFSDO, his organizational responsibilities include serving as an EIAFSDO Board Member , IT Department Admin and Web Admin .

His digital work is connected with website administration, technology systems, web development, digital infrastructure and the technical side of organizational platforms.

Alongside his EIAFSDO involvement, Priyabrata Patra is the founder of CodeBihin.in, an independent technology-oriented brand and web platform.

His professional contact identity is maintained through the CodeBihin domain using priyabrata@codebihin.in .
