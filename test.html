Who We Are

About EIAFSDO

We are an ambitious student-operated organization fostering collaborative engineering, project building, and knowledge exchange.

East Indian Amateur Full-Stack Developers Organization (EIAFSDO) sprouted from a close-knit group of inquisitive engineering students at HETC.

Guided by our foundation “Aedificare — Disputare — Crescere”, we believe real skill develops when theory meets practical implementation and peer feedback.
